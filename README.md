# Tawheed Worksheets (Grades 3–4)

Two worksheets on Tawheed, following the understanding of the Salaf as-Salih:

- **Tawheed ar-Rububiyyah**: believing that Allah alone is our Rabb (Creator, Provider, Owner, Controller)
- **Tawheed al-Uluhiyyah**: worshipping Allah alone

Each worksheet has a short lesson with Qur'an and hadith evidence, then fill in the blanks, matching, multiple choice, true/false, short answers and a drawing activity.

## What's here

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `pdf/` | Printable worksheets (one sheet, front and back) |
| `web/` | Interactive versions students can fill in on a tablet or computer, with a "Check my answers" button |
| `index.html` | The start page for the GitHub Pages site |

Live site: https://oddevenings.github.io/tawheed-worksheets/

Students' answers save in their own browser only. The answer keys and build scripts are kept privately by the teacher and are not in this repository.

## Sources

The worksheets are based on *The Foundations of Faith in the Light of the Qur'an and the Sunnah* (A Committee of Scholars), Section One, Chapters One and Two.
