Tawheed ar-Rububiyyah

Believing that Allah alone is our Rabb (Lord)

Let's Learn

Tawheed ar-Rububiyyah means to believe that Allah alone is the Rabb (Lord) of everything. Only Allah creates, provides (gives rizq), owns everything, and controls all things: He gives life and causes death, sends the rain and makes the sun rise.

No one shares any of this with Allah: not a prophet, not an angel, not an idol, and not the sun or the stars.

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

"All praise is for Allah, the Rabb of all the worlds." (al-Fatihah 1:2)

اللَّهُ خَالِقُ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ

"Allah is the Creator of all things, and He is the Guardian over all things." (az-Zumar 39:62)

Important Lesson

The disbelievers of Makkah also said that Allah created them (az-Zukhruf 43:87), but they still worshipped idols, so it did not save them. Believing Allah is our Rabb must lead us to worship Allah alone. That is Tawheed al-Uluhiyyah.
